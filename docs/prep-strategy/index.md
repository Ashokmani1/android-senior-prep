# Prep Strategy

Senior/Lead interviews are won on **judgment**, not recall. Pages here:

- **[How to Clear the Loop](how-to-clear.md)** — the anatomy of a Senior/Lead loop, what each
  round grades, and the answer structure that scores.
- **[4-Week Plan](study-plan.md)** — a concrete schedule that pairs this kit with your existing
  fundamentals resource.
- **[Manifest Book Coverage](manifest-coverage.md)** — cross-reference of a popular fundamentals
  book against this kit's modules.
- **[Coding Rounds & Company Questions](coding-rounds.md)** — a company-by-company question map
  (Booking.com, Spotify, PhonePe, Paytm, Meesho) plus worked Kotlin solutions for the DSA-lite
  problems some loops still include.

!!! quote "The mindset shift"
    Junior: *"I know how to use it."*
    Senior: *"I know when to use it, when not to, and what it costs."*
    Lead: *"I can align a team on that decision and own the outcome."*
